Join Our Lab

Everyone involved in each project is an integral part of the team. We encourage both local and international undergraduate and graduate students and postdoctoral fellows who are interested in learning more about our research to contact us to discuss opportunities.

Accomodation support for students studying at universities in Taiwan

We can provide private, free dormitories for students from other universities across Taiwan who come to Taipei for conducting short-term accommodation experiments. This allows students to leverage the vast and advanced resources at IAMS to conduct well-designed experiments without burdening them. In addition, we actively encourage collaborations with interested students and academics at other research institutions.

For more information on living in Taipei see Living in Taipei.

Living in Taipei

Taiwan is not just a global technology leader and the heart of the world's advanced chip industry. It is also one of the safest and most free places to live in the world. Taiwan's economy boast one of the highest GDP per capita (PPP) in the world with a comparable standard of living to the Netherlands and Denmark. Taiwan has a very low tax rate and one of the best and most efficient public health care systems in the world.

Average cost of living in Taipei:

  • Rent for an apartment (100 square meter): US$500-700/month
  • Rent for a single room (12 square meter) with shared bath and kitchen: US$300/month
  • Food for one person (eat out): US$300/month
  • Food for a small family (cook yourself): US$400/month
  • Transport costs: US$60/month

Climate of Taipei

  • Temperature: 18 degree C (winter) to 35 degree C (summer)
  • Humidity: 70% (winter) 80% (summer)
