Quantum Interfaces

Transferring quantum information between light and matter. Two lectures in the TIGP course Recent Trends in Atomic and Optical Physics, November 2026.

The two lectures

Matter stores quantum states and photons carry them. A quantum interface moves a state between the two, and it is what joins quantum processors, memories and sensors into a network. Lecture 1 builds the physics, from one atom in a light beam to an atom in a cavity and the gate a cavity makes possible. Lecture 2 takes the platforms that realise an interface and the links that have been made between them.

What the lectures teach

  • How much of a light beam one atom can take, and what sets the limit
  • How many atoms, a waveguide and a cavity each raise the coupling
  • How the coupling makes a memory, a source of single photons and a gate
  • How two distant nodes are entangled through photons, and at what rate
  • How ions, atoms, colour centres and circuits compare on one set of figures of merit
