01

Degree Programs

Undergraduate

Undergraduate Project

No experience needed — students have joined from robotics, optical, electrical and mechanical engineering as well as physics and chemistry. Projects are tailored to your interests, working closely with the team, ideally leading to published articles and conference presentations. See our projects.

Stipend 6–12k NTD/month · accommodation support available
Masters

Masters Project

Available in all our main research directions. Start alongside the team, then take charge of projects, guide junior students, publish internationally, and spend time with collaborating groups in Europe, North America and Japan. We recommend a smaller intern/undergrad project first.

Stipend 10–20k NTD/month · accommodation support available
PhD

PhD Student

Take charge of a large project and a significant part of a research direction — aiming for world-class results publishable in Nature, Science, PRL or PRX. We strongly recommend an experimental AMO masters or internship first. The TIGP offers comprehensive PhD support.

TIGP support program + more with experience

03

Research Jobs

Fellowship

IAMS Junior Fellowship

A prestigious fellowship for researchers transitioning from postdoc to professor — propose and lead your own research program with a host lab. Guidelines · IAMS info.

Applications open each August
Postdoc

Postdoctoral Fellow / Staff Scientist

Play a key role guiding research in the group. For experienced AMO experimentalists with a PhD; theorists interested in collaborating are very welcome. Send your CV and publication list — funding and visas take ≥ 3 months.

Support 60–84k NTD/month depending on experience
National Service

Alternative National Service

Complete your National Service as a full-time researcher on one of our research projects. Contact us for details.

Full-time researcher role
Hiring

Research Assistant

Strong background in experimental physics or engineering; preference to those with experience in our lab or related AMO labs. IAMS recruitment page.

Salary 38–56k NTD/month by qualifications & experience

Life & Visiting Fellows

Short / Long Term

Visiting Fellow

We invite established academics to consider short and long term sabbaticals in our lab. Contact us to discuss opportunities.

TW Students

Accommodation Support

Private, free dormitories for students from other universities across Taiwan coming to Taipei for short-term experiments — leverage the vast resources at IAMS without administrative hurdles.

An Advanced Economy

A global technology leader and the heart of the world's chip industry — one of the safest, most free places to live, with one of the highest GDP per capita (PPP). Low tax, efficient national health care, 24-hour convenience.

Culture

Minutes from the lab: hundreds of exquisite, inexpensive restaurants — Taiwanese classics and cuisine from across the world. One of the best places to learn Mandarin.

Natural Beauty

"Formosa" — beautiful. Rainforests, rugged mountains and beaches a short trip away, with 100+ peaks over 3000 m.
