Privacy Policy

BPMClaw Bot · Effective 23 September 2026

Who operates this tool

BPMClaw Bot is operated by the IAMS Ytterbium Quantum Technology Lab (Institute of Atomic and Molecular Sciences, Academia Sinica, Taipei) for its own members. Contact: s.p.bennetts@g.iams.sinica.edu.tw.

Which Google account it accesses

The tool accesses one Google account: the lab's own automation mailbox. It does not access the Google accounts of individual lab members or of anyone else. Lab members interact with the tool by emailing that mailbox or by using the lab's private messaging bot.

What data it uses and why

  • Gmail (read, modify, send) on the lab automation account: to read purchasing emails and quotations sent to it by lab members, to mark them as processed, and to send drafted purchase paperwork back to the lab member who requested it.
  • Google Sheets and Google Drive: to read and update the lab's purchasing ledger and purchasing-form reference spreadsheets, and to store copies of quotations.

Where the data is kept

Processing runs on the lab's own computers in Taipei. Copies of emails, quotations and drafts are stored on the lab's own file servers and Google Drive. Text from quotations may be sent to a third-party language-model API (Anthropic) to draft purchase paperwork; that text is limited to what is needed for the draft and is covered by the provider's commercial data-use terms, which exclude training on customer data.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, share or publish personal data.
  • It does not access any Google account other than the lab automation account.
  • It does not place orders or make payments. A named lab member reviews and submits every purchase request.
  • It is not offered to the public.

Retention and deletion

Purchasing records are retained for as long as the institute's procurement rules require. Lab members can ask the operator to delete drafts and copies of their emails at any time using the contact address above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

BPMClaw Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
