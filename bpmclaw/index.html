BPMClaw Bot

Internal purchasing-automation tool of the IAMS Ytterbium Quantum Technology Lab, Institute of Atomic and Molecular Sciences, Academia Sinica, Taipei.

What it does

BPMClaw helps laboratory staff prepare purchase requests for the institute's procurement system. It reads quotations and order records that lab members send to the lab's own automation mailbox, drafts the purchase paperwork, and returns the draft to the requesting lab member for checking. A human always verifies and submits.

Who uses it

Members of the IAMS Ytterbium Quantum Technology Lab only. The tool is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Google account access

The tool signs in to the lab's own automation Google account to read incoming purchasing emails, send drafts back to lab members, and read and update the lab's purchasing spreadsheets on Google Sheets and Drive. Details are in the privacy policy.
