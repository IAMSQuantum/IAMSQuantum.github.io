BPMClaw Bot · Effective 23 September 2026

1. Scope

BPMClaw Bot is an internal tool of the IAMS Ytterbium Quantum Technology Lab (Institute of Atomic and Molecular Sciences, Academia Sinica). It is provided only to current members of the lab who have been authorised by the principal investigator. Nobody else may use it.

2. Purpose

The tool prepares draft purchase paperwork from information supplied by lab members. Drafts are proposals only. Every purchase request is reviewed, corrected where needed and submitted by a named lab member in accordance with the institute's procurement rules. The tool never places orders or makes payments.

3. Responsibilities of users

  • Send only purchasing information that you are authorised to share with the lab.
  • Check every draft before you act on it. Automatically drafted text can contain errors.
  • Do not forward drafts to suppliers or to anyone outside the lab without the principal investigator's approval.

4. Data

Handling of data is described in the privacy policy.

5. No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for internal convenience, without any warranty of accuracy, availability or fitness for purpose. The lab may change, suspend or withdraw it at any time.
